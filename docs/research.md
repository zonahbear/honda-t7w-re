### S15 assessment
- **Supplier: Pioneer vs Fujitsu Ten.** S15 said Pioneer, and **S16 (Honda's own parts diagram for the 2016 HR-V EX) confirms the EX unit is marked (PIONEER)** — part `39100-T7W-A01…`. So the US EX T7W is a **Pioneer** build, **not** the Fujitsu Ten MY15ADA of S1 (which is a different-market HR-V). This is the first hard fork between the US unit and the S1 lead, and it downgrades how much S1/S2 (Fujitsu Ten, Tegra, Android 4.0.4) carry over. Note Pioneer was also the OEM behind the US 2015 Fit / 2014 Civic era HondaLink "Launcher" DA, consistent with S5/S9. The LX trim stays Panasonic (S10); EX is Pioneer.
- **SoC: Renesas/Telechips vs Tegra.** This conflicts with S2 (Tegra OMX libs from an HR-V Honda Connect). Same caveat: S2 isn't from a US unit.
- **"Linux-based OS".** Not actually a conflict, since Android is Linux-based. It doesn't help decide anything.
- **RAM 512 MB-1 GB.** No source. UNKNOWN stays UNKNOWN.
- **"Can't run CarPlay/AA, can't be modified".** This is a conclusion, not a fact, and it skips Approach A′. In A′ the dongle handles the phone protocol, Apple authentication and the radio. The head unit only has to decode an 800×480 H.264 stream, show it, and pass back touch input. S1 shows a same-generation HR-V unit doing exactly that. Whether the T7W *allows* third-party code is the real blocker (see hardware.md "UI survey"), not raw horsepower.
- **How to settle it:** a bench-spare teardown (next-steps, Status) reads the chip markings directly. Settles supplier and SoC in an afternoon.
